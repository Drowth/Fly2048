# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Added WoW Forever support (Interface 16001) with its own `Fly2048_Forever.toc`.
- The TOCs now cover Retail plus progression Classic, Titan-Reforged, Classic Anniversary and Classic Era.
- Fixed the player appearing twice on the guild leaderboard on realms with a space in the name.
- Frames only request BackdropTemplate on clients that have it.
- Removed the hidden corner buttons and their tunes, and the add-on is about 1.2 MB smaller as a result.
- Added weekly automatic TOC Interface bumps, tagged releases to CurseForge, and CI tests.

## 1.2.0-beta.1

- Rebuilt the interface with Blizzard frame art, panels and buttons.
- The board is a bag of item slots; tiles are loot icons with item-quality borders and bold numbers.
- Alliance and Horde themes replace Arcane and Ember, defaulting to your faction. Saved themes migrate automatically.
- The flightboard shows the leader crown on rank 1 and class-coloured guild names.
- Esc closes the window, and Blizzard sound effects now play correctly on Retail.

## 1.1.0-beta.1

- Added Retail Midnight 12.1 support through `Fly2048_Mainline.toc`.
- Added Retail AddOn Compartment controls.
- Added Arcane and Ember themes with value-specific tile palettes.
- Reworked the frame, score presentation, board, buttons, leaderboard, and game-over panel.
- Added adaptive tile text contrast and high-value milestone treatments.
- Added merge-chain feedback, score-card rollups, leaderboard pulses, and restrained pressure effects.
- Added reduced-motion mode, UI scaling, and saved window position.
- Preserved the existing best score, guild scores, mute setting, and taxi auto-popup setting.
